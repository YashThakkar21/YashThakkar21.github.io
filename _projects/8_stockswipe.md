Most people never start investing because the first screen of any brokerage is a wall of tickers, charts, and jargon. StockSwipe reframes discovery as something you already know how to do: swipe. Swipe right to invest, swipe left to skip, and flip a card over for a plain-language, AI-generated breakdown of the company plus its latest news.

### Features

- **Swipe-based discovery.** A card deck of stocks with a familiar right/left gesture, turning "where do I even begin" into a stream of one-at-a-time decisions.
- **Flip for AI analysis.** Each card flips to reveal an AI-written summary of the company, its fundamentals in human terms, and why it might or might not fit.
- **Live news.** Real, current headlines attached to each stock so a decision reflects what's happening now, not a stale snapshot.

The goal is an on-ramp: make the first investment feel approachable, then let curiosity pull people toward the deeper tools once they've started.
