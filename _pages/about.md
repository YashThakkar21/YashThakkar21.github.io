I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at **Princeton University** (B.S. expected May 2027), minoring in Mathematics and Robotics. My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning research** and **large-scale systems**: training agents that reason under uncertainty, and building the infrastructure that keeps such systems reliable at scale.

In April 2026 I joined the [Princeton Reinforcement Learning Lab](https://princeton-rl.github.io/), advised by [Prof. Ben Eysenbach](https://ben-eysenbach.github.io/) and [Mahsa Bastankhah](https://sites.google.com/view/mahsabastankhah/home), where I work on reinforcement learning and decision-making.

This summer I'm interning at **Google** as a Software Engineer in Mountain View, building a C++ service that detects orphaned Spanner databases across petabyte-scale Google Workspace recovery workflows. Previously I interned at **LinkedIn**, where I engineered an AI recruiting agent on an MCP server that automated 12 recruiter workflows, and before that at **Fidelity Investments** and **NoTraffic**.

On the research side, I've worked on [multi-agent reinforcement learning](/projects/judgement-rl/) for imperfect-information games, [better search for LLM reasoning](/projects/tree-of-thoughts-search/) (A\* and MCTS in place of Tree-of-Thoughts' BFS/DFS), and [evaluating whether LLMs reason semantically](/projects/llm-semantic-reasoning/) or lean on surface cues.

I also spend a lot of time teaching. As **Head Teaching Assistant** for Princeton's introductory CS sequence, I lead TA operations across three courses supporting 300+ students a semester, which is exactly what pushed me to build [TigerHelp](/projects/tigerhelp/), an office-hours platform I'm developing for our course staff.

Outside of that: drones, robotics, and finding excuses to write more C++.
