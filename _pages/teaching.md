## Head Teaching Assistant

**Princeton University, Department of Computer Science** · September 2025 – Present

I lead TA operations across three introductory computer science courses, supporting **300+ students per semester** in **Java** and **C**. The role spans two halves: teaching, and running the machinery that lets other people teach.

### Teaching

I work with students on **data structures**, **systems programming**, and — the part that matters most — debugging. Most students arriving in intro CS have never been taught how to isolate a fault. They've been taught syntax. A large share of office-hours time goes to replacing "my code doesn't work" with a hypothesis, a way to test it, and the next hypothesis.

### Operations

As Head TA I coordinate staffing, logistics, and instruction quality across course staff. Office hours at this scale are a queueing problem, and running them on spreadsheets meant students waited without knowing their position and TAs claimed the same question twice.

That's what motivated [**TigerHelp**](/projects/tigerhelp/), which our course staff now runs on: a live queue over Server-Sent Events, Princeton CAS authentication, and a TA analytics view that turns staffing from a guess about which weeks are "the hard ones" into a decision based on the actual distribution of demand.
